Proctored Exam Arena Rules

Verification requires absolute integrity. Please read and accept the guidelines.

⚠️ Any security breach or deviation automatically flags your profile and disqualifies you from badging.
  1. Strict Fullscreen Lock: The assessment is taken in full-screen. Navigating outside or exiting full-screen triggers a proctor breach and immediate failure.
  2. WebRTC Camera Feed: Your camera must remain active. AI sensors check for visual alignment and single-person frame environment compliance.
  3. Microphone Audio Sensor: Audio feed tracks decibels. External voice assistance or sound anomalies are automatically flagged.
  4. Whole Screen Sharing: You must share your entire primary display view. Shifting windows or opening other browser tabs is strictly blocked.
  5. Editor Controls Blocked: Copying, pasting, cutting, and right-clicks are programmatically disabled inside the workspace.

How it works

        
Challenge → Submit → Badge.

        
A student claims a competency. To verify it, they take a 3-tier shuffled coding test (Easy ➡️ Medium ➡️ Hard). Advanced AI evaluates it, issuing a certified badge tag on their public portfolio.

      

      

        

          
Step 01

          

            
          

          
Claim a competency

          
Select master programming skills like Python, C, or SQL. Rating is self-claimed until tested.

        

        

          
Step 02

          

            
          

          
Auto Shuffled Verifier

          
Our verification engine shuffles 3 progressive coding questions (Basics ➡️ Concurrency ➡️ Complex Slab Allocations).

        

        

          
Step 03

          

            
          

          
Secure Live Proctoring

          
Full screen locking, microphone noise tracks, WebRTC cameras, and anti-copy layers protect integrity.

        

        

          
Step 04

          

            
          

          
AI Evaluator & Summarizer

          
Generates senior engineer score rubrics alongside full proctor reports for recruiter portfolios.

        

        

          
Step 05

          

            
          

          
Tag and Badge Issued

          
Pass all 3 levels to earn custom tags (e.g. "SkillProof Python Expert") that recruiters trust.

        

        

          
Step 06

          

            
          

          
SkillProof Score Syncs

          
Dynamic talent aggregate score index recalculates on the user's secure server record.
